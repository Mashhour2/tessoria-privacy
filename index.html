Privacy Policy for Tessoria

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tessoria: Block Puzzle handles information when you use the game.

Information We Collect

Tessoria does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or user data.

Local Game Data

Game progress, scores, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to us or to third parties. Removing the app or clearing its data may delete this locally stored information.

Accounts, Advertising, and Analytics

Tessoria does not require an account and does not currently include advertising, analytics services, or in-app purchases.

Permissions and Internet Access

Tessoria does not request access to sensitive personal information. The game is designed to function without transmitting user data over the internet.

Children's Privacy

Tessoria does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the game's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
